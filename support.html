How do I move and shoot?

Put your left thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen and drag to move. Movement is instant: no drift, no sliding. Hold the big green FIRE button (bottom right) to shoot. Just hold it to auto-aim at the nearest target, or drag from it to aim yourself. Tap DASH to dodge; you're invulnerable mid-dash. Prefer less thumb work? Turn on Auto-Fire in the pause menu.

Game controllers work too (left stick moves, right stick aims, A dashes, B fires your special), and so do iPad keyboards: WASD to move, arrow keys to aim and fire, Space to dash, E for the special, Esc to pause.

What's a SUPER dash?

An orange ring fills around the DASH button as your operator's special charges. When it's full, the button turns orange and says SUPER, and your next dash fires the special: Sarge lands with a Ground Pound, Scryer looses a ring of homing arrows, Tinker drops a sentry turret and Vanguard raises a healing shield bubble.

Who can I play as?

  • Sarge, Orc Juggernaut: tough, with a water-cooled Wand-Gatling.
  • Scryer, Elven Recon: fast and fragile, with a piercing Scrying Bolt Rifle.
  • Tinker, Gnome Sapper: a ricocheting Rune Rivet Gun and sentry turrets.
  • Vanguard, Paladin Pointman: slow and sturdy, with a Breach Shotgun and a big shield.

Tap RAID! on the main menu to open the operator select screen, pick a hero, then tap DEPLOY.

I ran out of health. Now what?

If you have a 1-UP, you float up in a bubble and pop back in a moment later with full health. You start each raid with two, and green 1-UP mushrooms turn up from shell chains and "?" crates. With no 1-UPs left, the raid ends and your coins are banked.

How does a floor work?

Walk into a room and the doors lock behind you. Clear every wave (and break any rift portals) to unlock them. Clear the room with the elevator in it, then step on the green pad to go up. Every sixth floor is a boss.

Tricks worth knowing

  • Look for glowing beams of light: orange means a power weapon, green means an ammo box. They drift to you when you get close.
  • There are seven power weapons (Dragon-Head RPG, Necro-Nailgun, Arcane Claymore, Holy Flashbang, Thermite Launcher, Dreamtime Boomerang and Djinn Storm Wand), each with limited ammo. Grab a different one and your old weapon drops at your feet, so you can swap back.
  • Shoot the glass windows for a blow-out: the wind drags WyrmCorp staff right out of the building.
  • Walk into (or shoot) a Shellback's shell to kick it. It bowls over everything it hits, and chains pay big.
  • Orange canisters explode. Point them at enemies.
  • Shoot the orange ? crates for coins, weapons, ammo and power-ups.
  • Power-ups call in other units: Frenzy Surge, Bat Swarm, Shikigami, Dreamtime and Djinn Storm.

What's the Armory?

Mana coins you collect are banked at the end of every raid. Spend them on Toughness, Firepower and Super upgrades for each hero, or collect chase variants: rare paint jobs, just like real designer toys.

What's free, and what does the unlock include?

All of Act 1 (WyrmCorp Tower, six floors including the Chairwyrm boss fight) is free. A one-time Unlock Full Game purchase opens the Luna Mining Domes, the Shard Worlds, both remaining bosses and endless Tours after that. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

I bought the game. How do I restore it on a new device?

Tap Unlock Full Game on the main menu, then Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I turn off music or sound?

Use the ♪ and SFX buttons on the main menu, or pause during a raid (top right) and toggle Music and SFX.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app erases it. Your full-game unlock is tied to your Apple Account, so you can always restore it.
